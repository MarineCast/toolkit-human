HUMAN DOMAIN ROADMAP
====================

Shared Contracts
----------------

[ ] Require authoritative WA/BC and cross-border source provenance, licensing,
    attribution, temporal coverage, spatial coverage, and completeness before
    promoting any planned family from placeholder to production.

[ ] Implement every planned family with download.py, build.py, inspect.py,
    config.py, DATA_SOURCES.md, fixture tests, manifests, and feature-catalog
    entries; never replace unknown source coverage with zero.

[ ] Review model eligibility and temporal leakage for every new predictor.
    Coverage, provenance, QC, support, and reporting-adoption fields remain
