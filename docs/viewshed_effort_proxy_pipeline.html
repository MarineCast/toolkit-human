What this pipeline measures

End-to-end flow

Pipeline diagram

Read each lane left to right. The first lane creates the shared static kernel; the land and water lanes branch from it; the final lane shows the existing feature-table handoff.

data or configuration input transformation / validation durable artifact research or promotion boundary
Lane 01 Shared physical viewshed

Canonical nine-stage workflow from raw surfaces to durable pair tables.

Inputs

Area, support & rasters

Model bbox; territorial water; Natural Earth land; USGS 3DEP DEM; ETH canopy; canonical viewshed config.

Step 1

Download / validate surfaces

Acquire or reuse the regional 30 m DEM and canopy height, retaining checksums and source metadata.

Step 2

Build land source cells

Intersect H3 R7 cells with land/water support and apply configured active-fraction eligibility.

Step 3

Build source–target lookup

Create unique land-source and water-source candidate pairs to water targets within 30 km.

Step 4

Distance diagnostics

Compute centroid distance and the normalized logistic weight for land and water pairs.

d50 = 9 km · slope = 2.7 km · cutoff = 30 km
Step 5 · land

Dual-surface terrain + canopy LOS

Run paired GDAL viewsheds on bare earth and canopy-obstruction surfaces for 1.7 m observers and 1.0 m targets.

vegetation = min(Kcanopy, Kbare) / Kbare
Step 6 · water

Sampled land-mask LOS

Use three deterministic source and target samples, opaque buffered land, a 2.5 m small-craft eye height, and a 1.5 m target.

Step 7 · water

Neutral vegetation factor

Vegetation is not applicable to the water-source land-mask model, so its retained factor is exactly 1.

Step 8

Finalize static lookups

Validate unique keys, factor coverage, bounds, config hashes, and sidecars; materialize land and water tables.

static viewability = terrain × vegetation
Step 9

Export maps & clean workspace

Write aggregate/selected-location HTML maps and run manifest, then remove rebuildable data/tmp/viewshed/h3r7 intermediates.

Final factor: weight_static_viewability = weight_terrain × weight_vegetation.
Diagnostic only: weight_distance is retained but is not multiplied into the static score a second time.
Production canopy path: canopy LOS is active; the optional ESA WorldCover attenuation pipeline is not used.
Lane 02 Land reporting opportunity

Physical viewability combined with accessibility, human context, weather, daylight, and calendar support.

Inputs

Land kernel + context tables

Land static weights; transport; population travel; public shore access; HRRR weather; daylight; calendar; manifests and inventory.

Step 10

Validate identities & coverage

Check raw inventory and upstream checksums, routing-policy agreement, static scientific hash, and partial-source opt-in.

Step 11

Assemble source components

Require exact H3 R7 source universes for transport and population; left-join shore evidence; retain unavailable and unknown states.

reachability = population travel × road proximity
Step 12

Propagate static components

Multiply each source component by each source→target physical weight and sum by H3 R7 target.

target raw = Σsource(static weight × source component)
Step 13

Prepare dynamic basis

Map sources to weather R5/daylight R4, bin distance every 0.25 km, map targets to H3 R6, and conserve pair mass.

Step 14

Compute daily opportunity

Apply distance-aware meteorological visibility, daylight, wind, precipitation, and—on human streams—the calendar multiplier.

physical daily = static × visibility × daylight × wind × precip
Step 15

Publish component streams

Keep physical, population, road, city, transport, reachability, mapped-access, and verified-access streams separate.

Step 16

Aggregate Monday weeks

Sum daily raw values; retain day counts, status, and context coverage; compute fixed-reference q99 inspection indices.

Step 17

Publish immutable generation

Write source R7, target R7, daily R6, weekly R6, metadata, and supplement; validate; atomically replace the root manifest selector.

Canonical stream: LAND_OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITY_RAW is a partial mapped-public-access lower bound; LAND_EFFORT_PROXY_RAW is its schema-v1 alias.
Strict sensitivity: VERIFIED_LAND_OBSERVATION_OPPORTUNITY_RAW uses authoritative verified-public evidence only.
Null contract: missing weather, access, or routing context remains unavailable; it is not assumed to be zero.
Lane 03 Water observation opportunity

Daily and weekly class components; no canonical primary composite.

Inputs

Water kernel + daily activity + conditions

Water static weights; daily H3 R6 AIS classes; native H3 R7 ferry rider/platform activity; R5 weather; R4 daylight; manifests.

Step 10

Checksum-bind local inputs

Require explicit partial-data opt-in, immutable ferry/AIS manifests, complete weather, deterministic daylight, and the current viewshed scientific hash.

Step 11

Prepare two propagation kernels

Use the documented uniform-within-parent R6 AIS kernel while retaining native-R7 ferry propagation before R6 target aggregation.

R6 kernel = Σ R7 pair weights / (source children × target children)
Step 12

Keep activity classes separate

Publish all-vessel, passenger, recreational, commercial (towing + cargo + tanker), fishing, ferry rider, and ferry platform components.

Step 13

Apply dynamic viewing conditions

Apply distance-aware atmospheric visibility while keeping daylight, wind, precipitation, and coverage as separate fields.

component raw = Σsource(activity × static viewability × visibility × daylight × wind × precipitation)
Step 14

Preserve unavailable states

Retain positive partial contributions; emit zero only under complete coverage. Sea state, whale-watch, and reporting capture remain null / source_unavailable.

Step 15

Transform without probability claims

Publish raw, log1p, and same-period spatial-rank variants. Define no canonical primary water score.

legacy passenger + recreational AIS = compatibility/sensitivity only
Step 16

Publish immutable schema-v2 generation

Write source/target daily and weekly tables, lineage, sightings and WA/BC diagnostics, then validate the HTML report before advancing the selector.

Activity unit: AIS remains a unique MMSI-hour proxy; ferry retains rider-hours and platform-hours independently.
Causal metadata: ferry is generally exogenous; passenger/all AIS are mixed; recreational AIS is potentially endogenous.
Promotion: every new component is research-only and model-ineligible; the ecological estimator is unchanged.
Lane 04 Modeling feature handoff

The current code path for research features and shadow evaluation.

Input

Active land weekly generation

Resolve the root manifest, require exactly one weekly artifact, verify upstream checksums and config identity.

Step 18

Validate modeling grain

Require unique Monday + H3 R6 keys, nonnegative raw values, coverage bounds, and seven-day completeness for comparable raw sums.

Step 19

Build fixed-support panel

Reindex the full Monday × model-cell calendar; keep missing cells missing; calculate fixed-denominator regional lower bounds.

Step 20

Create lagged features

Generate previous-week, rolling-4-prior, and rolling-13-prior raw and coverage features with availability counts.

Step 21

Feature blocks / shadow output

Expose baseline, coverage, composite, components, and combined blocks. Served predictions remain incumbent; candidates stay explicit.

Boundary

No automatic promotion

Current results are mixed, historical context is contemporary, and production integration plus coverage gates remain open.

Model values: raw opportunity enters the estimator; q99 indices are inspection products.
Leakage control: all lags use completed prior weeks; no same-week or future-period value is used.
Water handoff: schema-v2 class components are research-only and have no promoted model consumer or primary composite.

Interpretation

Contracts that must survive every join

These boundaries are more important than the convenience names “effort” and “viewshed.”

Physical viewability

A modeled geometric opportunity to see a water target from a source cell.

  • Not a calibrated detection probability.
  • Not evidence that an observer was present.
  • Static pair weights remain separate from dynamic weather and activity.
Reporting-opportunity proxies

Relative, partially observed conditions under which reports could be generated.

  • Land: access/reachability lower bounds × physical/dynamic viewing support.
  • Water: class-specific AIS or ferry activity × physical and dynamic viewing support.
  • Neither is direct observer-hours, vessel pressure, or whale truth.
Availability semantics

Unknown, unavailable, unmapped, partial, zero, and not-applicable are distinct states.

  • Unknown access is not zero-filled.
  • Incomplete AIS target-weeks remain null unless a positive contribution is observed.
  • Model features mask partial calendar weeks rather than comparing partial sums.

Feature tables

What is model-ready today

The repository has feature tables and adapters, but “available” is not the same as “promoted.”

Land: explicit modeling adapter exists

The active weekly generation feeds modeling.py, which validates the manifest, builds a fixed-support panel, and exposes lagged feature blocks. Only raw opportunity and coverage values are eligible; q99 indices remain for inspection.

Water: component tables exist; no model adapter

Daily and weekly tables expose independent AIS classes, ferry rider/platform opportunity, viewing conditions, controlled states, and lineage. Every feature is cataloged as research-only and model-ineligible. The ecological estimator was not changed.

Current land feature blocks

baseline, coverage, composite, components, and combined, built from previous-week, rolling-4-prior, and rolling-13-prior values plus availability counts.

Promotion remains blocked

Evaluation is mixed; six HRRR source-parent cells are absent; sightings coverage does not support regional non-report calibration; and production champion integration plus prospective shadow monitoring remain open gates.

Knowledge-time warning: static access, population, routing, and shore evidence are contemporary context applied retrospectively. They support research comparisons, not strict historical operational replay before those inputs existed.
